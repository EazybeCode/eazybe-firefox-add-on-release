Installing

  1. Choose Add to Firefox above.

    Firefox will ask you to review the permissions the add-on needs.

  2. Confirm with Add.
  3. Open web.whatsapp.com.

    The Eazybe sidebar appears beside your chats. Sign in with your Eazybe account to connect a CRM.

The file downloaded instead of installing

Open about:addons, click the gear icon at the top right, choose Install Add-on From File, and pick the downloaded .xpi.

Why isn't this on addons.mozilla.org?

This build is distributed directly by Eazybe while the Firefox listing is being prepared. It is signed by Mozilla, so it installs in Firefox exactly like an add-on from the store.
